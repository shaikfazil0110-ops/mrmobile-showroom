Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our grading system

How is battery health measured? +
We use professional diagnostic tools to measure battery capacity percentage. This indicates how much charge the battery can hold compared to when it was new. A 100% battery health means the battery is brand new or nearly new, while 70% means it can hold 70% of its original capacity.
Can I return a device if I'm not satisfied with its grade? +
Absolutely! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If the device doesn't match the described grade, we'll either replace it or provide a full refund. Your satisfaction is our priority.
What's the difference between B and C grade devices? +
Grade B devices show moderate cosmetic wear but are fully functional with 80-90% battery health. Grade C devices have more visible wear (scratches, marks) but are still perfectly functional with 70-80% battery health. Both work flawlessly - the difference is mainly cosmetic.
Do all devices come with warranty? +
Yes! All devices come with extended warranty coverage. A+ grade: 24 months, A grade: 12 months, B & C grade: 6 months. Additional insurance options are available at checkout.
Is an A+ graded phone worth the extra cost? +
It depends on your priorities! A+ devices are essentially like-new and have 24-month warranty. If you want maximum protection and peace of mind, A+ is worth it. If you're budget-conscious, B or C grades offer excellent functionality at lower prices.
How often do you update the grading criteria? +
Our grading criteria are reviewed quarterly and updated based on industry standards and customer feedback. We maintain the highest transparency and consistency in our grading process across all devices.
